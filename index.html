Big Two

4-player card game.

Rules

  • Ranks: 3 < 4 < … < K < A < 2.
  • Suits: ♦ < ♣ < ♥ < ♠. Everything except Flush and Straight Flush compares rank first, then suit: singles, pairs, three of a kind, straights, full houses, four of a kind (♦2 beats ♠A; same rank: ♠ beats ♥). For pairs/trips the highest-suit card breaks ties.
  • Hand sizes: 1, 2, 3 or 5 cards. Four-card plays are illegal.
  • Follow the count: answer a single with a single, a pair with a pair, and so on. After three passes in a row the last player leads anything.
  • First turn: holder of ♦3 starts and must play it.
  • 5-card order (low→high): Straight < Flush < Full House < Four of a Kind < Straight Flush.
  • Straight: five consecutive ranks (no wrap-around, so A-2-3-4-5 is not allowed); compares its highest card, rank first then suit.
  • Flush and Straight Flush: suit first, then highest rank (any ♠ flush beats any ♥ flush). Full House: only the three-of-a-kind counts (rank first, then suit). Four of a Kind: rank of the four.
  • Winning: first to empty their hand. Losers lose one point per card left; the winner gains the total.
